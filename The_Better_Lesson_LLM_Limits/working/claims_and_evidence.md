# Claims and Evidence Ledger

Status: active epistemic ledger.

This ledger prevents philosophical proposals, empirical findings, design
hypotheses, and source attributions from silently changing status. Claim
identifiers should remain stable even when wording is revised.

## Status vocabulary

- `conceptual`: a philosophical or analytic proposal requiring argument rather
  than direct empirical confirmation.
- `adopted thesis`: the author's governing explanation, to be argued by the
  article rather than withheld pending universal empirical proof.
- `adopted proposal`: the article's proposed remedy or design direction.
- `empirical hypothesis`: testable but not yet established here.
- `source-supported`: supported as an attribution to an identified source.
- `supported`: evidentially supported for project use.
- `open`: insufficiently specified or evidenced.
- `rejected` or `superseded`: retained for provenance but not current.

## Promotion rule

A claim moves to `supported` only when the relevant source or test is recorded
in `research_summaries.md`, its limitations have been considered, and the
project explicitly adopts the resulting judgment in `current.md`. Repetition
in conversation does not change a claim's status.
